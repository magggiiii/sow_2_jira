Loaders

Motion loaders ported from beui.dev/components/motion/loader to dependency-free vanilla CSS + JS, themed to the app's accent. Try your OS "reduce motion" setting — each one falls back to a calm opacity pulse.

Scramble
Text reveals left-to-right; unrevealed slots flicker glyphs.
Helix

Newton
Five-ball cradle; the two end balls swing, middle three rest.
